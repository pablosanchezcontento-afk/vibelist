What data we use

  • Your request (text and options) is sent to a free AI provider (Pollinations) to suggest songs. We don’t keep a history of requests.
  • Spotify: if you connect your account, the permission is stored only in your browser so we can create playlists for you. We never see your password or email. You can revoke access in your Spotify account settings.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card; your browser only keeps the purchase code Stripe returns.
  • Music catalogues: your browser queries iTunes, MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz to verify songs and find similar artists.
  • Local storage: your browser keeps your Pro licence, today’s free-playlist count, your language and your Spotify session.
