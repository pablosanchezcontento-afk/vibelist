The service

Vibelist builds song lists from your request and lets you save them to your Spotify account or export them. Vibelist does not stream Spotify content and is not affiliated with Spotify or Apple.

Free and Pro

The free plan has daily limits. Pro is a monthly subscription of €1.99 (VAT included) processed by Stripe. It renews every month until you cancel; you can cancel anytime from “Manage subscription” and keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Keep the code shown after payment to restore Pro in another browser.
